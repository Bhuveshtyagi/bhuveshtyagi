Venture Deep-Dive · Founded 2025

Orgelix Vermicompost

The Agricultural Problem

Excessive reliance on synthetic chemical fertilizers has depleted organic carbon levels in agricultural soil across Northern India. Orgelix was founded in Meerut to provide accessible, 100% organic soil nutrients that regenerate microbial health.

Biological Process & Quality

Using specialized Eisenia fetida earthworm cultures and strict moisture-controlled bed management, agricultural waste and cow manure are bio-converted into pure, odor-free, pathogen-free humus rich in NPK, humic acid, and micronutrients.

Distribution & Scaling

• Direct-to-farm bulk supply for fruit orchards and wheat growers
• Premium retail packaging for home gardeners and nursery partners
• 15+ tons of agricultural biomass upcycled to date
