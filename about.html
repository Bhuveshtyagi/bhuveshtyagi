01 / Foundation

The Pursuit of Technical Depth

My journey into technology began with a deep fascination for the internal machinery of computers — how high-level code compiles into bare-metal operations, and how thoughtful software design simplifies human problems.

As a 1st-year BTech Computer Science and Engineering student at SRM Ghaziabad, I balance foundational CS theory (data structures, systems programming in C++, discrete math) with hands-on full-stack development. I focus on standard web primitives, zero-dependency efficiency, and scalable architectures.

02 / Commercial Realism

Building Real Ventures: Uniimize & Orgelix

Code alone does not solve business problems without distribution and product clarity. Alongside my academic coursework, I actively operate two enterprises:

1. Uniimize: A strategic branding and design agency partnering with businesses to formulate bespoke visual identities, typography guidelines, packaging, and digital interfaces.

2. Orgelix: A sustainable vermicompost and bio-fertilizer enterprise producing 100% organic soil nutrients from agricultural waste, serving regional farmers and green spaces in Uttar Pradesh.

03 / Social Commitment

Free UI/UX Design for NGOs

I do not accept commercial freelance contracts. I believe time outside university and venture operations should serve meaningful impact.

Pro-Bono Commitment: I offer my UI/UX design, design system development, and web consultation 100% free of charge to verified NGOs and non-profit organizations dedicated to education, healthcare, rural empowerment, and environmental preservation.
